# Research, October 2026: self-transversal designs, `ε(n)`, and FK-A's subproblems

Results of the program in [`research-prompt.md`](research-prompt.md). Each item is a
*Theorem*, *Proposition*, *Lemma* or *Observation* proved in Lean, or a *Conjecture* stated with
its evidence and search bound. Lean names refer to
[`lean/HypergraphDualization/`](../lean/HypergraphDualization); `lake build` in `lean/` checks all
of them. Vertices are 0-indexed. *Observation* means a finite fact proved by evaluation
(`native_decide`), which trusts the Lean compiler as well as the kernel.

Notation. `U` is the ground set, `H` a hypergraph inside `U`, `Tr(H)` its minimal transversals.
`H` is *intersecting* if any two distinct edges meet, *2-colourable* on `U` if some `A ⊆ U` has
no edge inside `A` and none inside `U \ A`.

## 1. The Witt design `S(4,5,11)` (Track A)

`w11` is the instance `data/instances/w11.json`: 11 points, 66 blocks of size 5.

**Observation 1.1** (A1). `w11` is a Steiner system `S(4,5,11)`: every 4-set of points lies in
exactly one block. `Tr(w11) = w11`. Two distinct blocks meet in 1, 2 or 3 points, for 495, 660
and 990 unordered pairs respectively.
Lean: `w11_steiner`, `w11_self_tr`, `w11_meet`.

**Theorem 1.2** (A2). `|Aut(w11)| = 7920`, and `Aut(w11)` acts sharply 4-transitively, hence
primitively, on the 11 points.
Lean: `card_aut_w11`, `w11_four_transitive`, `w11_four_free`, `w11_preprimitive`.

*Proof.* Let `ι : Aut(w11) → (Fin 4 ↪ Fin 11)`, `σ ↦ (σ0, σ1, σ2, σ3)`. Of the 5040
permutations fixing `0, 1, 2, 3` only the identity preserves `w11` (`stab_check`), so `ι` is
injective. For each of the `11·10·9·8 = 7920` ordered 4-tuples `t` of distinct points, the
product `wit t = u₀u₁u₂u₃` of Schreier transversal elements (generated by
`experiments/witt_m11.py`) preserves `w11` and sends `(0,1,2,3)` to `t` (`witCheck_eq`), so `ι`
is surjective. Hence `|Aut| = 7920`; the action on 4-tuples is transitive and free; a
2-transitive action is primitive (Mathlib). ∎

By Jordan's theorem a sharply 4-transitive group of degree 11 is the Mathieu group `M₁₁`. This
identification is cited, not proved in Lean.

*Remark (computation).* `witCheck` iterates the block list and compares blocks as bitmasks. The
first version, written with `Finset` membership and `Decidable (∀ e ∈ L, _)`, ran for more than
10 minutes; the `Decidable` instance of a bounded quantifier over a list costs about 70 times
`List.all` under `native_decide`.

**Correction 1.3** (F2, re-derived by `python experiments/witt_m11.py`). At the root FK-A splits
on `v0`. The deletion child has 36 blocks of size 5; the contraction child keeps the 36 blocks
avoiding `v0` and the 30 four-sets, 66 edges. Both have `|Aut| = 720` on the 10 active points,
and both act primitively. The archived order 1440 is `|Aut S(3,4,10)|`, the derived design, not
an FK-A node. (Python; the Lean statement is item A6 of the program.)

## 2. Self-transversal hypergraphs

**Proposition 2.1** (A3). Let `H` be Sperner with every edge inside `U`. Then `Tr(H) = H` iff
`H` is intersecting, `∅ ∉ H`, and `H` is not 2-colourable on `U`.
Lean: `tr_eq_self_iff`.

*Proof.* By Lemma 2.1.1 with `G = H`, `Tr(H) = H` iff for every `A ⊆ U` exactly one of `A` and
`U \ A` contains an edge. "At most one" for all `A` is the conjunction of: `H` intersecting
(take `A = e`) and `∅ ∉ H` (take `A = ∅`). "At least one" for all `A` is non-2-colourability. ∎

The Sperner hypothesis is needed: `H = {{0}, {0,1}}` on two points satisfies the right side but
`Tr(H) = {{0}}` (`tr_eq_self_iff_needs_sperner`).

**Theorem 2.2** (A4). Let `H` be a nonempty, intersecting, `r`-uniform hypergraph (`r > 0`) inside
`U`, `|U| = t`, in which every point of `U` has degree `d`. Then
`d(r² − t) ≥ r(r − 1)`, with equality iff distinct edges meet in exactly one point.
Lean: `intersecting_regular_bound`.

*Proof.* Let `b = |H|`. Counting incidences, `br = td`. Fix `e ∈ H`. Counting pairs `(x, f)` with
`x ∈ e ∩ f`, `rd = Σ_{f∈H} |e ∩ f| = r + Σ_{f≠e} |e ∩ f|`, and each of the `b − 1` terms is at
least 1. Hence `d(r² − t) − r(r − 1) = r(Σ_{f≠e}|e ∩ f| − (b − 1)) ≥ 0`, with equality iff every
term is 1. ∎

Self-transversal uniform regular hypergraphs are intersecting, so the bound applies to them;
`steiner_selftr.py` finds equality on `K₃` and the Fano plane, strict inequality on `w11`,
`Maj₅`, `Maj₇`, `Maj₉`. The step from equality to "projective plane of order `r − 1`" is not
proved here.

## 3. The least most-frequent-vertex frequency `ε(n)` (Track B)

`ε(G, H) = max_v max(deg_G v/|G|, deg_H v/|H|)` for a transversal pair, both sides nonempty and
without the empty edge; `ε(n)` is its minimum over pairs on `n` points.

**Conjecture 3.1** (B2, solver evidence). `ε(6) = 1/2`, `ε(7) = 3/7`, `ε(8) = 2/5`.

*Evidence.* `python experiments/eps_search.py` encodes "some pair on `n` points has every
frequency below `p/q`" as a 0/1 program on the truth table of a monotone `f : {0,1}ⁿ → {0,1}`
(`2ⁿ` variables, minterm indicators for `f` and its dual, degree ordering to break the `Sₙ`
symmetry) and solves it with OR-Tools CP-SAT:

| `n` | bound | CP-SAT | time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | `ε < 2/3` | infeasible | < 0.1 s |
| 4, 5, 6 | `ε < 1/2` | infeasible | < 0.1 s |
| 7 | `ε < 1/2` | feasible, a pair with `ε = 5/11` | 0.3 s |
| 7 | `ε < 3/7` | infeasible | 0.1 s |
| 8 | `ε < 2/5` | infeasible | 646 s |

The upper bounds are attained: `1/2` by `{{0,3},{1,2}}` with isolated points added (Lean, on four
points: `eps_four`), `3/7` by the Fano plane (`fano_eps`), `2/5` by 8-Ver. The lower bounds rest on
CP-SAT's infeasibility verdicts, which are not Lean certificates; Lean proves only
`ε(3) = 2/3` and `ε(4) = 1/2` (`eps_three`, `eps_four`).
